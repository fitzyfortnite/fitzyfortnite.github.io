Privacy Policy

Effective

In short: Escape From Granny has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your progress and settings stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID — only as described below and, where the law requires it, only with your consent.

1. Who we are

This policy covers the mobile game Escape From Granny (“the Game”), published by (“we”, “us”). If you have a question about privacy, write to .

2. Information we keep on your device

The Game saves your progress (floors reached, notes found, achievements, lives), your settings (language, sound, graphics, frame rate) and whether you own “Remove ads”. This is stored only in the Game's local storage on your device. We never receive it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not removed ads, the Game shows interstitial and optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, if you allow it);
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps).

Your choices. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the Game shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested; you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. On iPhone and iPad the system asks whether the Game may track you; if you decline, your IDFA is not shared. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings. Without consent, ads are non-personalized.

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases

“Remove ads” (a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime unlock) is sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle payment; we never see your name, card or billing details. The Game only receives a confirmation that you own the item, so it can turn ads off and restore your purchase.

5. Achievements

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, the Game reports unlocked achievements to that service. This is governed by Apple's or Google's privacy policy.

6. Children

The Game contains horror themes and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the Game in a way that shared their data, contact us and we will help.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, the fastest way is to use the in-game Privacy choices, your device's ad settings, or Google's tools. You can always contact us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Retention and security

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the Game. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policy. The Game sends no data to us, so there is nothing for us to store or breach.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the Game.

I bought “Remove ads” but I still see ads

Open the store panel from the main menu and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy.

How do I cancel the monthly subscription?

iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the Game does not cancel it.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

In the Game, open Settings → Privacy choices. On iOS you can also change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved only on your device. It is lost if the Game is uninstalled. Finished floors stay unlocked in Floor select as long as the Game is installed.

I'm stuck on a floor

Tap Get a hint under the objectives (available every 10 seconds) or open Tasks to see every task on the floor in order. Code doors show where their code is hidden.
